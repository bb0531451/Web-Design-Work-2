I want to make learning fun for students. I think that the best way to teach kids is by having activities and making the learning fun and engaging. I want to have interactive activities that will be geared towards whatever we are studying that week. I think that history is super fun, but I also understand that there are those who don’t believe that. My goal as a teacher is to make learning history fun and help them succeed in the class by making the activities also include helping them remember the topic and what we learned.
